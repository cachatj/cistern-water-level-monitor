Outdoor node wiring

Drawn from the updated README (Oct 7, 2026). Pins use the labels printed on the board (D18, VIN). The small text under each one gives the GPIO number used in code and where the pin sits on the board. The sensor wiring is the same in both modes. Only the power source and the top of the battery divider change.

Where the terminals are on your adapter

The other labels are GPIOs too. VP = GPIO 36 and VN = GPIO 39, both input-only. TX0/RX0 = GPIO 1/3 carry USB serial, so leave them free. TX2/RX2 = GPIO 17/16. EN is the reset line.

Field junction board (one EPLZON board)

On this board, holes A–E in each numbered column are connected to each other, and so are F–J. Each of those 5-hole strips works as a small rail. A colored strip below is one connection point. Check two holes in the same strip with continuity mode before soldering: they should beep. The ESP32 stays on its screw-terminal adapter, and the TP4056 and U3V16F5 mount on the enclosure plate as modules.

Wired directly, not through this board: JSN-SR04T Trig → D5 terminal, TP4056 OUT+ → U3V16F5 VIN, panel → TP4056 IN+/IN−, cell holder → TP4056 B+/B−. Use red wire for 5V, orange for 3V3 and black for GND so the strips are easy to tell apart.

Check before power-on

  1. Set the multimeter to continuity and check each terminal you use (D5, D18, D27, D34, VIN, 3V3, GND) against its pin on the ESP32 itself. Each pair should beep.
  2. Echo goes to the 1 kΩ resistor, never straight to D18.
  3. Measure the battery-divider junction with a multimeter before wiring D34. Expect ≈2.4–2.5 V. It must be under 3.3 V.
  4. Find the rain gauge's switch pair with continuity mode while tipping the bucket. It is normally the center two RJ11 conductors.
  5. Aim the ultrasonic probe at a target at least 25 cm away. Closer readings fall in its blind zone.
  1. Holder leads read ≤ 4.2 V with both cells in. A reading near 7–8 V means the holder is wired in series.
  2. Panel open-circuit voltage in full sun is ≤ 6.0 V. The TP4056 absolute maximum is 6.5 V.
  3. U3V16F5 output reads 4.8–5.2 V before the ESP32 is connected.
  4. Load grounds go to OUT−, not B−. Otherwise the protection circuit can't disconnect the load.
  5. With everything connected, measure deep-sleep current in series with the battery. Expect roughly 15–20 mA.

The 3.3 V rail comes from the ESP32's own 3V3 pin in both modes. Only the 5 V source changes.

Changed from the rev 1 drawing

  • Echo now passes through a 1 kΩ / 2 kΩ divider. The 2 kΩ is a 2.2 kΩ and a 22 kΩ in parallel, both from the kit. Echo no longer connects straight to D18.
  • A U3V16F5 boost regulator sits between TP4056 OUT+ and the 5 V rail. The battery no longer feeds VIN directly.
  • The 1N5819 diode is gone. The solar cable is soldered straight to TP4056 IN+/IN−.
  • System ground is TP4056 OUT−. B− and IN− go only to the cells and the panel.
  • The rain gauge (D27 + 10 kΩ pull-up) and the bench OLED are now drawn.
